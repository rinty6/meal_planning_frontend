Snack and drink slots, and planning around today

Checklist m5-04. This is design only. Nothing in the app or the backend has changed yet. There are four decisions, each with a recommendation, and they're collected at the end.

2026-11-19 · Measured on the live catalogue pool (1,845 plate foods, 443 of them allowed at snack time today)

What m5-04 adds, in one paragraph The planning screen gets a fourth tab, Snack, with five snack ideas a day. A snack is one or two foods from the five food groups (your rule from 2026-11-16), never a cake or chips. Snacks take the 10% of the day they already have in the meal split, and they carry a quarter of the day's fruit and dairy. That is the piece m5-03 could not reach with plates alone. Then the planner starts reacting to what you've already logged today: if breakfast had no dairy, lunch, dinner and the snack lean toward dairy.

What changes, and what doesn't
  • Four tabs instead of three. They're the same tabs as today, with the label at 14 px instead of 16 px, because "Breakfast" in bold at 16 px is about 80 px wide and each of four tabs gets about 76 px on a 375 px phone. The count reads "5 ideas" so it fits under every label.
  • The snack card is the plate card. It uses the same DishCard, the same two-photo cluster from round 1 (one circle per food), the same detail page with editing, and the same one-log-row-per-food. The only difference is a green SNACK label, using the snack green #15803D from m4-09.
  • One food or two. The cards above are all pairs. A handful of almonds alone is a snack too (decision 2).
  • The grey hint line appears only on a meal whose ideas were adjusted for what you've already logged (section 3). It never shows serves (m0-06).
  • Energy figures are for a woman 19–50 on about 2,000 kcal a day, so a snack is about 200 kcal (840 kJ). Plates outside the ±25% window are dropped, as for meals.
Lunch and dinner get about 10% smaller Today the three meals share the whole day, because the snack's 10% is spread over them (25/30/35 becomes 28/33/39). Once the snack is planned, the meals go back to 25/30/35 and the snack takes its 10%. The day's total doesn't change. This is also when the m5-03 serve pattern stops rescaling its snack column over three meals.

3Planning around what you've logged today

You chose to do this together with snacks. Today the planner ignores the log completely. Lunch ideas are the same whether breakfast was porridge with milk or nothing at all.

You
Log breakfast (any way: plate, barcode, search, voice), then open the planning screen.
App
Asks for the plan with today's local date, which the route already accepts (?date=).
Server
Reads today's logs and adds up what's been eaten: energy from every row, and serves from catalogue rows. Barcode and FatSecret rows have no food groups, so they count as energy and zero serves. This errs toward suggesting a little more of a group, which is the safe side, because serves are minimums.
Server
Spreads what's left of each food group over the meals with nothing logged yet, using the m5-03 meal pattern.
Server
Does the same for energy, but gently: an unlogged meal is never planned below 80% or above 120% of its usual size (decision 4).
App
Shows the adjusted meals with the grey hint line. Meals you've already logged keep their usual ideas, in case you want to add more.

4Drinks

The checklist says drinks are first-class (m0-04), and you've had a water target since m4-06 (2.6 L for men, 2.1 L for women). There are two different jobs here, and only one belongs to the planner:

  • Drinks with nutrition (milk, calcium-fortified soy) are dairy. They're already in the snack pool, as in the last card above, and in the pair list.
  • Water, tea and coffee have no energy and no serves. Nobody needs a recommendation to drink water. What they need is a running total against 2.1 L. That is a tracking screen, and it's already m6-02 ("Water needs a running total against the target").
  • Juice, cordial, soft drinks, sports drinks and flavoured milk are never suggested. Juice is "occasionally" in the ADG. The others are discretionary. They can all still be logged.

5Decisions

D1 · Snack contents
  1. Recommended: the table in section 2. Keep fruit, dairy, nuts and crackers. Add raw vegetable sticks. Remove cakes, dairy desserts, fruit dishes, corn snacks and bars. Add the half-serve rule. This edits the approved meal table's snack column only. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are untouched.
  2. Keep the snack column as approved and rely on the half-serve rule alone. Scones and toffee apples would still pass.
D2 · What a snack card holds
  1. Recommended: one or two foods. One food when it reaches the snack's energy on its own at a normal portion (a handful of almonds, a tub of yoghurt). Otherwise a pair from the list in section 2, with the first food chosen from the group today still lacks.
  2. Always one food. Simpler, but an apple is 90 kcal against a 200 kcal snack, so most fruit would either fail the energy window or show up as "2 apples".
D3 · Drinks
  1. Recommended: milk and fortified plant milk are suggested as dairy snacks. Nothing else is suggested. The water running total stays in m6-02.
  2. Add a drink line to every plate ("+ a glass of water"). It's always the same line, so it carries no information and adds clutter to every card.
D4 · How hard energy reacts to what's logged
  1. Recommended: food groups react fully. Energy reacts within 80–120% of each meal's usual size.
  2. Food groups only. Energy stays fixed, so a big breakfast is followed by full-size lunch and dinner.
  3. Both react fully. After a big breakfast, dinner could be planned at 200 kcal, and most plates would fail the energy window.
Build order once these are decided
  1. Backend snack builder: meal table edit, half-serve rule, pairs, 5 snacks in the plan payload. Tested with numbers before anything is drawn.
  2. Backend "today": read the logs, work out what's left, the 80–120% clamp, and a cache key that changes when you log.
  3. App: the fourth tab, the green SNACK label and the hint line. This brings forward m6-01 ("Four slots on the planning page"), because without it the snack can't be tested on the iPhone.
