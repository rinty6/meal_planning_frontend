How it behaves
  • The drink row sits under the foods line on every plate and snack card. It shows the plate's first drink suggestion.
  • + Add turns it on, and the headline energy then includes the drink ("with drink"). Tapping again turns it off.
  • On the plate detail page there are chips: None plus up to 3 drink options from the server. The default is None unless the card's toggle was on.
  • Picking the plate logs one extra row for the drink, in the same meal and with source "catalog", only when the drink is on.
  • The plate's foods are built without the drink. A drink with energy is offered only when the plate leaves room for it (see the rules).
  • A whole dish on its own is labelled DISH instead of "PLATE · 1 FOOD".

Rules
  • Never suggested: soft drinks, cordial, fruit drinks, sports and energy drinks, flavoured milk, mocha, juice with added sugar, smoothies, dry tea leaves, lemon or lime juice, and alcohol (m0-07).
  • Juice: 100% juice only, at most 250 ml, and on at most one of the five cards per meal. The ADG counts 125 ml as a fruit serve, "only occasionally".
  • Energy: a drink over 40 kcal is offered only on a plate at or below 95% of its energy target. Other plates get water, tea or a black coffee.
  • Caffeine: no coffee for users under 18. During pregnancy or breastfeeding, coffee is offered at breakfast only, and decaf elsewhere.
  • Allergen and diet filters apply to drinks exactly as to food (milk in a flat white).
